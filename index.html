Understand your privacy

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Does the app work without internet?+

Yes. Core itinerary planning and saved trip details remain available offline. Live weather, exchange rates, online map details, theme downloads, and some sharing actions require a connection.

Do I need to create an account?+

No. You can begin planning immediately without registering an account.

Can I share a trip with someone?+

Yes. Export a portable .vacaytrip file through Email, Messages, AirDrop, or Files. Private Wallet records, personal settings, diary entries, and photos are excluded.

How does the free version work?+

The free version supports one active trip. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited trips.

Which languages are available?+

English, Japanese, Vietnamese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Spanish, Italian, and French.
